UP Scholarship Login – Complete Student Guide

The UP Scholarship Login portal is an essential online service provided by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. It is designed to help students from all backgrounds, including SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and General categories, to apply for educational scholarships efficiently. This platform enables students to register, submit fresh or renewal applications, track verification progress, and receive scholarship payments directly in their bank accounts.

Purpose of the UP Scholarship Portal

The main goal of the UP Scholarship system is to provide a centralized digital platform for managing all scholarship-related activities. By eliminating the need to visit government offices, the portal ensures transparency, reduces paperwork, and accelerates application processing.

  • Register and apply for scholarships online
  • Update personal, academic, and bank details
  • Upload required documents securely
  • Track application progress in real time
  • Receive scholarship payments directly to bank accounts

What is OTR and Why It Is Mandatory?

OTR, or One Time Registration, is a mandatory process introduced by the UP Government for all scholarship applicants. Each student must complete OTR before logging into the scholarship portal. This system allows a one-time data submission that simplifies future applications.

Benefits of OTR

  • One-time data submission for all future years
  • Faster processing of fresh and renewal applications
  • Reduced errors and duplication of records
  • Easy tracking of all student scholarship records

After completing OTR registration successfully, students receive a unique OTR ID, which is required for logging into the UP Scholarship portal.

How to Login to UP Scholarship Portal

Students can follow these steps to access their scholarship dashboard:

  • Visit the official UP Scholarship website
  • Click on Student Login
  • Select Fresh Login or Renewal Login
  • Enter your OTR ID / Registration Number
  • Provide your password and captcha
  • Click Login to access your account

For official student login and step-by-step guidance, visit: UP Scholarship Student Guide.

Fresh vs Renewal Applications

Fresh Applicants: These are students applying for the UP Scholarship for the first time. They must complete OTR registration, fill personal and academic details, upload all necessary documents, and submit the application before the deadline.

Renewal Applicants: These students have received scholarships in previous years. Renewal involves updating academic information, confirming previous records, submitting the renewal form, and checking payment status.

Documents Required

  • Aadhaar Card
  • OTR ID
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Previous year mark sheet
  • Bank account passbook
  • Institution registration details

How to Check Scholarship Application Status

Once logged in, students can track their application through the dashboard:

  • Application submission status
  • Institute verification
  • District-level approval
  • Scholarship payment release

Forgot Password?

Students who forget their password can reset it online using the Forgot Password option. Verification will be done through the registered mobile number.

Important Guidelines for Students

  • Use only the official UP Scholarship portal
  • Do not share login credentials with anyone
  • Verify bank account details carefully
  • Submit applications before the deadline
  • Keep a copy of the submitted application for future reference
