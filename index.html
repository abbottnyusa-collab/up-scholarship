UP Scholarship Login – Official Student Portal for Fresh & Renewal Applications

The UP Scholarship Login portal is an online student service managed by the Social Welfare Department, Government of Uttar Pradesh. This portal allows eligible students to apply for scholarships, complete OTR (One Time Registration), submit application forms, track verification status, and check scholarship payment details online.

The UP Scholarship scheme supports students from economically weaker backgrounds, including SC, ST, OBC, Minority, and General category students studying in recognized schools, colleges, universities, ITIs, and professional institutions across Uttar Pradesh.

Purpose of UP Scholarship Login Portal

The UP Scholarship Login system provides a centralized digital platform where students can manage all scholarship-related activities without visiting any government office. It ensures transparency, reduces paperwork, and speeds up the application and verification process.

  • Register and apply for scholarships online
  • Update personal, academic, and bank details
  • Upload required documents
  • Track application progress
  • Receive scholarship amount directly in bank account

What is OTR and Why It Is Mandatory?

OTR (One Time Registration) is a compulsory process introduced by the UP Government for all scholarship applicants. Every student must complete OTR once before logging in to the scholarship portal.

Benefits of OTR

  • One-time data submission for future years
  • Faster application and renewal process
  • Reduced chances of errors and duplication
  • Easy tracking of student records

After successful OTR registration, students receive a unique OTR ID, which is required for UP Scholarship Login.

How to Login to UP Scholarship Portal

  1. Visit the official UP Scholarship website
  2. Click on Student Login
  3. Select Fresh Login or Renewal Login
  4. Enter your OTR ID / Registration Number
  5. Enter password and captcha
  6. Click on the Login button

UP Scholarship Login for Fresh Students

Fresh applicants are students who are applying for the UP Scholarship for the first time. These include students who have taken new admission in the current academic year.

  • Complete OTR registration
  • Fill personal and academic details
  • Upload required documents
  • Submit application before the deadline

UP Scholarship Login for Renewal Students

Renewal applicants are students who have received scholarship benefits in previous academic years and wish to continue receiving financial assistance.

  • Update academic year information
  • Confirm previous records
  • Submit renewal form
  • Check scholarship payment status

Documents Required for Login & Application

  • Aadhaar Card
  • OTR ID
  • Income Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Domicile Certificate
  • Previous year mark sheet
  • Bank account passbook
  • Institution registration details

How to Check UP Scholarship Application Status

Students can check their scholarship application status after logging in to the dashboard.

  • Application submission status
  • Institute verification
  • District-level approval
  • Scholarship payment release status

Forgot UP Scholarship Login Password?

Students who forget their login password can reset it online using the Forgot Password option on the login page. Password reset requires verification through the registered mobile number.

Important Guidelines for Students

  • Use only the official UP Scholarship portal
  • Do not share login credentials with anyone
  • Verify bank account details carefully
  • Submit application before the deadline
  • Keep a copy of the submitted application
